Free and Premium Features

Tracking your money in FullWallet is free. That includes transactions, fixed costs, savings, budgets and their alerts, the Home Screen widget, and exporting and importing your data.

Premium adds:

  • Adding transactions by voice, in the App and from the Home Screen widget.
  • The AI Money Coach: one AI spending analysis every 30 days. Once you run an analysis, the next one becomes available 30 days later, and the App shows how many days remain.
  • On iPhone, logging Apple Pay purchases automatically through a Shortcuts automation that you set up.
